# Hashsite and Hashpath

Hashsite and Hashpath are sister projects.

## Hashsite

Hashsite is the map/app/resolver. It answers: where is this place, and how do I share or preview it?

Canonical site: <https://hashsite.org>

## Hashpath

Hashpath is the grammar/spec/docs project. It answers: how do we encode real-world arrival, access, delivery, pickup, and task instructions?

Canonical site: <https://hashpath.org>
