## Interop and companion work

Hashsite is the native format.

Companion tooling can sit beside it for interoperability, translation, migration, or critique of proprietary systems.

## W3WNKER

**W3WNKER** means:

```text
Worldwide 3D Wayfinding: iNteroperability Kernel for Emergency Response
```

It is a companion interoperability idea for reading W3W-compatible addresses and moving users toward open geocoding.

The purpose is straightforward: provide an offline, open interoperability layer so users are not trapped in a proprietary word-based location ecosystem.

Hashsite should remain the center of gravity. Interop tools should help people move into open geocoding, not deepen dependence on closed systems.

## Hashpath

Hashpath is the live companion product for multi-step real-world instructions:

<https://hashpath.org>

Hashsite gives places compact codes.

Hashpath carries procedures around places: parking, gates, doors, access codes, dropoff points, exits, and other arrival details.
