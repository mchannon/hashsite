## Companion work

Hashsite is the native format.

Companion tooling can sit beside it for interoperability, translation, migration, or critique of proprietary systems.

Hashpath is the live companion product for multi-step real-world instructions:

<https://hashpath.org>

## W3WNKER

**W3WNKER** means Worldwide 3D Wayfinding: iNteroperability Kernel for Emergency Response.

It is a companion interoperability idea for reading W3W-compatible addresses and moving users toward open geocoding.
